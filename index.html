OUR STORY – BUHI IntelliFarm idea started when ASF affected our community that led to a considerable number of abandoned pigpens. Having a background of Agricultural and Biosystems Engineering and Computer Science, our team as children of backyard farmers wanted to transform these underutilized and ASF-affected pig pens into biosecure, high-yield aquaponics facilities powered by continuous IoT water telemetry and real-time monitoring devices and automated system.

BACKGROUND & RATIONALE

An Inspiration from traditional fishing - Parugmon

African Swine Fever (ASF) left vast agricultural structures unused across rural communities. BUHÌ IntelliFarm addresses this crisis by transforming abandoned pig pens into clean, biosecure, intelligent aquaponics installations.

Initiated at Brgy. Bun-od, Cuartero, Capiz, our project minimizes labor, optimizes resource consumption, and promotes climate-resilient local food production through automated telemetry.

OUR STRATEGIC GOALS

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To establish BUHÌ IntelliFarm as Western Visayas' Premier Research, breeding, and demonstration hub for native freshwater species and sustainable smart agriculture.

Our Mission

To equip smallholder farmers with accessible, scalable IIoT technology that improves productivity, reduces manual intervention, and protects local ecosystems.

Value Proposition

Continuous automated water monitoring, dynamic filtration, and automated refilling ensure pristine conditions for aquatic life and premium crops.
