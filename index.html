Our Story

Young, new, and just beginning to grow.

BUHÌ started at home. When African Swine Fever (ASF) swept through our community in Brgy. Bun-od, Cuartero, Capiz, backyard pig pens across the barangay went silent and empty.

We are children of backyard farmers with a background in Agricultural and Biosystems Engineering and Computer Science. We saw those empty pens as an opportunity: turn them into biosecure, high-yield aquaponics farms run by real-time IoT monitoring and automation.

We don't just build the technology. We farm with it, and we grow real harvests on our own smart demo farm.

Project Parugmon

Inspired by parugmon, a traditional fishing practice, our demo farm is an aquaponics greenhouse converted from an ASF-abandoned pig pen. What was once neglected is now full of life: lettuce, pechay and other greens grow on recycled water from the fish below.

Parugmon is where every Agri-Bot is built, tested and proven, with less labor, less water, and climate-resilient local food production.

BeforeIdle, ASF-hit pig pen
AfterBiosecure smart aquaponics farm
